# Emberwake — Design Bible

> Power is immortal. When powerful things die, their power does not vanish — it pools in the world. You are a spark inside a human host. You grow by absorbing those pools. Growth always costs something.

---

## Premise (fixed)

- **Immortal power.** Death of great beings, empires, beasts, and champions leaves **elemental pools** in the land: fire in volcanoes and ashfields, water in seas and deep wells, lightning in storms and shattered peaks. Untended pools **rot** — they disaster the land.
- **The player is a spark**, not the host. You inhabit a human body (initially **Lira**) and ride her senses, choices, and battles. You feed by digesting pools. Healing the land is a **side effect of feeding**, not charity.
- **Combining elements** unlocks new forms of power (merge tree). Travel uses **waystones** (anchored pool-gates) and **eagles** (sky routes between high places).
- **Tone:** epic, beautiful, emotional, slightly dark. Wonder and grief sit together. Growth costs — every absorption leaves a mark on host, land, or conscience.
- **Audio:** open-licence only; credit every track and SFX in-game and in credits.

## Story spine

| Beat | Content |
|------|---------|
| Host | **Lira** — young scout of the border villages; strong will, unsure why the world answers her. The spark wakes in her after she touches a dying ember-pool. |
| Empire | The **Ashen Concord** bottles “safe” magic into seals, batteries, and licensed rites. They drain pools into vaults so citizens never see rot — and never see true fire either. |
| Rot | Unprocessed or mismanaged pools curdle into **rot**: blighted soil, screaming weather, hollow beasts. The Concord’s bottling *creates* rot downstream. |
| Player role | Digest pools → grow spark → host gains elemental fight paths. Land heals as byproduct of honest digestion. |
| Rival | **Vesper** (suggested name) — another spark in a Concord vessel. Absorbs greedily, spreads and worsens rot, sells “cleansed” land back to the Empire. Charismatic, tragic, mirrors the player’s hunger. |
| Combat paths | Host fight styles powered by elements: **Warrior** (body + fire/earth), **Mage** (mind + water/lightning), **Ranged** (focus + wind/hybrid). Paths hybridize as merges unlock. |
| Inventory | Lives on **host and party**, not the spark. Gear, consumables, and sealed shards travel with people. |
| Endgame | **Prime Remnant** — the first death that taught the world immortality of power; a continent-scale pool the Concord cannot bottle. Rival race; choice of true digestion vs dominion. |

## Timeline (acts)

### Act I — Ember in the Leaf
Wake as spark in Lira on **Verdant Isle**. Learn movement, first pool (ember), first rot patch, village reaction. Meet party seed. Concord patrol bottles a lesser pool in sight. Rival silhouette at act end.

### Act II — Bottled Sky
Cross by eagle/waystone to **Stormreach Coast**. Lightning pools, Concord vaults, first Warrior/Mage/Ranged branch choice. Vesper named; debate and duel (not lethal). Party expands. Merge tree opens (Fire+Water, etc.).

### Act III — The Rot That Speaks
**Ashen Marrow** — inland blight where bottled magic leaked. Moral weight: feeding heals, but overfeeding scars the host. Midgame dungeon: Concord digest-engine. Vesper’s method revealed.

### Act IV — Remnant Wake
World map opens; waystone network. Race for **Prime Remnant**. Faction endings hinge on how you merged, who lived, whether Lira remains host or becomes something else. Credits name all open-licence audio.

## Rival

**Suggested name: Vesper**  
Spark-sibling. Absorbs without digesting cleanly — power blooms, rot blooms harder. Believes the Concord’s bottle is mercy and the player’s hunger is vanity. Recurring boss / mirror fights; optional redemption or final ruin at Prime Remnant.

## First three regions

1. **Verdant Isle** — tutorial overworld (grass, village, dungeon mouth, ember pools). Soft beauty, first rot scars.
2. **Stormreach Coast** — cliffs, waystones, lightning, Concord harbor vaults, eagle roosts.
3. **Ashen Marrow** — inland blight, volcano ashfields, fire/earth merges, moral setpieces.

## Systems

### XP
- Spark XP from **digesting pools** (primary) and combat (secondary).
- Host class XP from combat actions matching path (Warrior/Mage/Ranged).
- Level-ups unlock pool capacity, merge slots, and path skills. Overcap digestion risks **host strain** (temp debuffs / story flags).

### Gold
- Local currency of the Concord rim. Buys gear, eagle passage, waystone tolls, and black-market unbottled shards.
- Some vendors refuse gold for true pools — trade favors or purified rot-ash instead.

### Inventory
- Stored on **host + party** (bags, packs, camp chest).
- Categories: consumables, equipment, **pool-shards** (undigested), **seals** (Concord bottles — dangerous to crack), key items.
- Weight / slot soft-cap; spark itself carries nothing.

### Equipment
- Weapons and armor keyed to path + element affinity.
- Affinity bonus when equipped element matches a digested pool type.
- Rare **Remnant fragments** as late crafting ingredients.

### Element merge tree (sketch)

```
Fire ──┬── Magma (Fire+Earth)
       ├── Steam (Fire+Water)
       └── Plasma (Fire+Lightning)

Water ─┬── Ice (Water+Wind*)
       ├── Tide (Water+Earth)
       └── Storm (Water+Lightning)

Lightning ─┬── Thunder (Lightning+Wind)
           └── Glass (Lightning+Earth)  [brittle, high crit]

Earth ─── Root / Quake (support merges)

Wind* ── travel + Ranged path (eagle synergies)

* Wind may be gated behind Kestrel / eagle quests.
Higher merges (e.g. Prime blends) locked to Act IV.
```

Merges are permanent spark knowledge; host must still equip or attune to express them in battle.

### Encounter / combat notes
- Overworld: random / proximity encounters; rot zones raise rate and add blight enemies.
- Turn-based party combat; clear turn order; Fight / Magic / Item / Flee (and later Element skills).
- Paths:
  - **Warrior** — stance, interrupts, fire/earth weapon skills.
  - **Mage** — pool-cost spells; water/lightning; can dump undigested shards for burst at rot risk.
  - **Ranged** — positioning, wind, marking; eagle call for limited sky-strike in open maps.
- Absorbing a pool mid-dungeon can change arena (heal tiles / clear rot / empower rival if contested).
- Flee fails more often in rot and against Vesper.

## Reference tone (locked)

- **Final Fantasy:** party cast, job/path identity, turn-based set-piece battles, overworld → dungeon → boss rhythm, emotional cutscene beats, mergeable magic as spectacle.
- **The Witcher:** grey politics, Empire as bureaucratic evil not cartoon, messy consequences, grounded dialogue, contracts/quests with no clean answer, rot as a world problem people argue about.
- **Not Zelda:** no shrine-puzzle toybox framing, no “fun gadget open world” fantasy as the pitch.

Combat feel: FF menus and party turns; narrative feel: Witcher moral mud.
