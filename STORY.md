# Emberwake — Story (player-facing)

Power does not die.

When empires fall, when dragons burn out, when champions breathe their last, what they were does not scatter into nothing. It **pools** — fire under volcanoes, water in the deep, lightning in the bones of storms. Left alone, those pools **rot**, and the land pays.

You are not the hero’s body. You are a **spark** waking inside **Lira**, a border scout who touched a dying ember and lived. To grow, you must **absorb** the pools of the world — and every feast leaves a cost on her, on the party that walks with her, on the places you pass through.

The **Ashen Concord** bottles magic into something “safe.” Their seals keep cities bright and fields quiet — until the rot they push away comes back louder. Another spark, **Vesper**, drinks as you do, but spreads blight in their wake and calls it cleansing.

Warrior, Mage, or Ranged: Lira’s fight is yours, fueled by the elements you digest. Waystones and eagles carry you from the soft green of **Verdant Isle** to storm coasts and ash-marrow wastes. Beyond them waits the **Prime Remnant** — the first great death, the pool that taught the world that power is immortal.

Feed. Heal as a side effect. Choose what you become.
